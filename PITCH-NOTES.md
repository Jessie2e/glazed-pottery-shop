# Glazed Pottery Shop — v2 Pitch Notes

## The new design idea

This version deliberately moves away from the traditional earth-tone artisan-shop look. The foundation is light, graphic and modern: lots of white/linen space, black typography and Glazed's existing teal spiral as the one strong brand accent.

The design borrows the *strategy* Mandy likes in Wolf Ceramics — product categories are immediate, photography gets room to breathe, shopping is never buried, and the maker/process story supports the products instead of taking over the homepage — while keeping Glazed visually distinct.

## What to show Mandy

1. **The header/logo treatment** — the horizontal Glazed logo now has a transparent background, so it floats cleanly over the sticky navigation instead of showing a white rectangle.
2. **The hero** — intentionally younger and more editorial, with bold condensed typography, rotating studio/product photography and two immediate actions: shop or book.
3. **The category browser** — uses the current Etsy structure and counts: All 33, Sponge holders 5, Chip and dips 4, Berry bowl / colander 4, Soap dishes 6, Mugs 3, Air Plant Hangers 3, Garlic Keepers 3 and Plates 1.
4. **The product cards** — minimal commerce-first cards with image swap, inventory badges and Quick Add.
5. **Raku + Glaze Lab** — the dramatic dark section is intentionally the exception to the light site. It makes Raku feel experimental and special while keeping the interactive mineral mixer.
6. **Classes / Studio / Membership** — one website becomes the front door for both product revenue and the local studio community.

## Etsy → Shopify wording

Avoid promising that everything will be scraped. Position the migration as:

> “We’ll move your existing product information and images into Shopify, then connect the custom storefront to Shopify so inventory, cart and checkout live in one system.”

This keeps the pitch accurate while leaving room to use Etsy exports, APIs or a migration tool depending on the final catalog.

## Important prototype note

Class dates, seat availability, membership pricing and a few product placeholders are interface examples until Mandy supplies final business details. Product categories and counts are based on the current Etsy category screenshot supplied for this redesign.
