# Glazed Pottery Shop — V3 Design Direction

## Core idea
A contemporary Colorado ceramic studio, not a generic artisan boutique and not a loud streetwear brand.

## Visual system
- Warm white / linen base
- Glazed teal as the single primary accent
- Charcoal only for contrast and the Raku / Glaze Lab moment
- Large photography with restrained UI
- DM Sans as the main typeface; Barlow Condensed only for small labels/eyebrows
- No decorative blobs, overlapping photo cards, or fake editorial clutter

## Hero
One full-bleed studio image with subtle scroll parallax. The official tagline is the headline: “Innovative pottery for the home.” No picture-on-picture treatment.

## Content hierarchy
1. Full-bleed brand hero
2. Trust / provenance strip
3. Product-first shop + quiet Etsy migration reassurance
4. Mandy’s actual design philosophy
5. Studio classes / reservations / membership
6. Dark interactive Raku + glaze test-tile lab
7. Existing Etsy social proof

## Source discipline
Copy is grounded in Mandy’s uploaded bio and current Etsy shop wherever possible. Marketing copy is kept restrained and is not presented as a Mandy quote unless it is actually hers.

## Glaze Lab
The old CSS mug was removed. The interaction is now a ceramic test tile with an oxidation/reduction toggle. Mineral controls are chemistry-inspired, not predictive; the UI explicitly says it is not a real formula or firing prediction.

## Story section refinement — V3.1

The Mandy quote and craft/process copy now share one horizontal editorial section instead of stacking as two large blocks. The quote sits in a warm-white panel; the craft story sits in a pale Glazed-teal panel with one restrained teal edge accent. This compresses the section, creates more visual rhythm, and makes the teal feel structural rather than decorative. On mobile the two panels stack with compact spacing.

## V3.3 refinement
The classes/studio experience now comes before the Form + Function story. The quote, functional product image, and process copy are consolidated into one editorial band immediately before the Raku / Glaze Lab section, reducing scroll length and strengthening the progression from community experience → design philosophy → material experimentation.
