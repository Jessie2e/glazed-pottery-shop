Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Thank you for shopping with Glazed Pottery Shop! Every piece is handmade with care, and we want you to love your pottery.

Handmade Pottery

Each item is individually crafted, making every piece unique. Slight variations in shape, size, color, texture, and glaze are natural characteristics of handmade pottery.

These variations are part of what makes handmade ceramics special and do not automatically indicate a defect.

Returns & Exchanges

Return and exchange eligibility varies by product. Please review the individual product listing for any specific return or exchange conditions before purchasing.

Items must be returned in their original condition to receive a full refund. This applies to eligible returns unrelated to damage, defects, or an incorrect item; those issues are handled under the section below.

Because many of our pieces are handmade to order, some items may not be eligible for returns or exchanges due to a change of mind.

If you have questions about a product's eligibility, please contact us before placing your order.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under applicable consumer protection laws.

Cancellations

Cancellations are not accepted once an order has been placed.

Because we often begin planning or creating pottery specifically for your order, please review your selections carefully before completing your purchase.

This does not affect any cancellation or refund rights provided by applicable law.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

We carefully package each order to help it arrive safely.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery, if possible, at glazedpotteryshop@gmail.com.

Please include your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photographs of the item and packaging when relevant.

We'll review the situation and work with you toward an appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, refund, or another solution depending on the circumstances.

The requested 48-hour reporting window helps us investigate shipping damage promptly and does not limit your rights under applicable law.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will generally be issued to your original payment method.

Please allow additional time for your bank or payment provider to process the refund.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any concerns about your order, please reach out. We're happy to help!
